_Ceratosaurus_ was (not surprisingly) a ceratosaurian theropod, which lived during the Late Jurassic of western North America. Living alongside the larger _Allosaurus_ and _Torvosaurus_, _Ceratosaurus_ could easily be distinguished by a prominent horn on its snout, smaller horns over the eyes, and a row of bony plates along its back. 

While _Ceratosaurus_ skeletons are less common than _Allosaurus_, we do have something of a growth series, with juvenile, subadult and larger, more robust adult specimens known. Although the largest specimen was originally described as a different species (_Ceratosaurus dentisulcatus_), it's generally recognized today as _C. nasicornis_, so is presented here as the older, fully grown stage of development.

The teeth of _Ceratosaurus_ were large for its size, which some have taken as an indication that it hunted relatively large prey, although others have interpreted the striated enamel of the teeth as evidence they were hunting lungfish, so it's safe to say there isn't a consensus yet beyond "not-plants". The mounted casts of the juvenile specimen have teeth that look truly enormous, but that seems to be due to them slipping out of their sockets after death - not only is this a common occurrence in vertebrates, but you can see on the specimen that much of what is exposed is root rather than the enamel-covered crown.
