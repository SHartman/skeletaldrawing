Hi Atharv - I'm glad you are enjoying the update, and looking to get back into paleoart. There isn't a one-answer-fits-all response to what beginners should work on, because it depends largely on what your current strengths are, and what your goals are. But learning more about anatomy (both the animals you want to draw, and living animals as well) is always good, as is learning about composition, lighting and different mediums. But most of all I'd recommend spending time practicing - time on task is the major way to get better at most anything, including paleoart!
