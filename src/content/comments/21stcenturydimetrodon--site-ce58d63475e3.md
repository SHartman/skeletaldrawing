Hi Joseph - Dimetrodon is basically only found in swamps, so it should rarely, if even be portrayed outside of water. That said, it's not really found in deep-water environments either, so while I'm sure it could swim if necessary (most animals can), I strongly doubt it was an underwater pursuit predator (it simply doesn't show the anatomy for such). I'd guess it either stalked through water, or waited for prey to come to it and snatched them. They not only feasted on amphibians and stem-tetrapods, but freshwater sharks as well.
