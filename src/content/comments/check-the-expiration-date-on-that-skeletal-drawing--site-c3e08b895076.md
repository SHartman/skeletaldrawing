Thanks Daniel - You've already read the blog, but I'll add that I remember having a moment of worrying about the dates back when I made the call to change how the images are watermarked, but I liked the design improvement and didn't really consider the issue again until it was raised after launch. In retrospect I suppose I should have realized I wasn't the only one making use of them!

The scale bars (and especially measuring lengths of the animals, rather than just reporting Wikipedia values) added quite a bit of time, but I think it makes everything more useful (although people should realize the length estimates here are for the specimen being restored; if there are much larger specimens known I try to mention them in the description below). That work will pay off in another way in a bit though, so it was worthwhile.

I will note that Baryonyx probably needs a once-over yet again, in light of the newer Suchomimus data, which clearly should be used to fill in missing gaps and guide the un-crushing of Baryonyx bones. Fingers crossed it will be soon(TM)!
